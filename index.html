Your voice, wherever you type.

Hold a key and speak in Bulgarian or English. Your words appear wherever you're typing. You can also turn meeting recordings into text.

Windows 10 or 11

Get started

Slovo works with a server run by someone you trust. You need an invite from them first.

  1. Install Slovo

    Download it above and run the installer.

  2. Ask for your invite

    The person who runs the server sends you a private invite link. Slovo uses Tailscale, a free app that makes a private, encrypted link between your device and the server.

  3. Open Slovo and follow the steps

    It guides you through installing Tailscale, signing in (or creating a free account) and accepting your invite. The server owner approves you once, and you're in.

Windows may warn you when you open the installer. Slovo is a small personal project and isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. If you see "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway.

How it works

Hold Right Ctrl to speak Bulgarian or Right Shift to speak English, then let go. The text is pasted into whatever you were typing in.

Privacy

The server processes your audio without saving it; your history stays on your device.

Private connection

Your audio travels over a private, encrypted connection (Tailscale) to the server. It is turned into text in memory, and the text is sent back to your device.

Nothing saved on the server

The server keeps no audio and no text. Your dictations and transcripts are stored on your device only, and nothing syncs between devices.

You are in control

Delete your history any time, or let Slovo delete it automatically. The server owner can see who connected and when, and how long the audio was, but not what was said.

Updates

Slovo checks GitHub for updates. GitHub can see your IP address, but your dictations, transcripts and settings are not sent.

Slovo is named after the Glagolitic letter Ⱄ, the first letter of слово, meaning “word”.
